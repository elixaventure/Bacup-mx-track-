How it works

Ride all day, no sessions

There are no timed sessions. Once you've signed on and paid, you can ride until we close. Marshals watch the track the whole time, and the rules below keep mixed riding safe.

Sign on

Every rider signs the disclaimer at the gate before going on track. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to sign, and that adult stays on site.

Kit check

We check every rider's helmet, boots, gloves and goggles. No full kit, no riding. See the kit list.

Ride your level

Beginners and kids keep to the outside line and roll the jumps. Faster riders pass with care and give everyone room.

Take breaks

Come off track in the pit lane only. Leave the track slowly with your hand raised so riders behind know you're stopping.

Rules and safety

Track rules

Kids and adults share the track, so these rules are not optional. Marshals can send anyone off for riding dangerously.

On track

  1. Ride in the direction of the track only.
  2. Never stop on a jump landing or blind corner.
  3. Pass slower and younger riders wide and with care.
  4. Obey every flag and every marshal straight away.
  5. No passengers, and no riding in the pits faster than walking pace.
  6. Bikes must pass our noise limit:dB limit TBC.
  7. No alcohol on site for riders.

Flags

Know your flags before you ride

Green

Track is clear. Ride.

Yellow

Danger ahead. Slow down, no jumping, no passing.

Red

Stop riding. Pull over safely and wait.

Black

You've been called off. Leave the track and see a marshal.

White

Medical or recovery vehicle on track. Ride with extreme care.

Chequered

Track closing. Finish your lap and come off.

Do I need to book?

No. Open riding is turn up and ride. Check the track status before you travel.

Can complete beginners ride?

Yes, as long as you have full kit and can control your bike. Keep to the outside line and roll the jumps until you're confident.
